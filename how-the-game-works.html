How the game works

Stroke play, Stableford and Wolf, scored automatically. Here is exactly how Honors keeps the card for your group.

The basics Stroke play Stableford Reading the card Wolf

The basics

One phone keeps the card for the whole group. Anyone in the group without the app is added as a guest (name and handicap) and the host’s phone keeps their score too. Everyone sees a live leaderboard.

Stroke play

Players are ranked by their score to par over the holes played. Lowest wins. Scores are shown as E, +3, −2.

Stableford

You earn points on each hole and the most points wins. Handicap strokes are given on the hardest holes, by stroke index.

Points = 2 + par + strokes received − your strokes, never below 0.

Reading the card

  • A score is drawn inside a circle when under par and a square when over par.
  • Two rings or two squares means two or more under or over (eagle or better, double bogey or worse).
  • A picked-up hole counts as double par.

Wolf

Wolf is a four-player game where one player each hole is the Wolf: they watch the others drive, then pick a partner or go it alone. Honors keeps every point for you, including the Wolf’s calls, rollovers and the Wolf Cap for the winner.
