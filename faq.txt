HONORS – FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
(Current copy from index.html)

----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Do I have to pay for early access?

No, early access is free! You’ll be among the first to play Honors, and you’ll get a say in how the app takes shape.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

2. What do you do with my information?

We use the information you provide to manage your early access and keep improving the app. We won’t share your information with third parties.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

3. What do I need to do with early access?

Nothing formal. Play a few rounds, set up a league with your mates, and if you’ve got thoughts on what we could do better, we’d love to hear them.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

4. How do I get the app on my phone?

Sign up using the form below and we’ll email you everything you need to get the app on your iPhone. Early access runs through Apple’s official TestFlight program, so you’ll download the app from a reliable source.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

5. Is the app available for Android?

Right now early access is iPhone only. Once we’ve got that to a good place we’ll be launching an Android version.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

6. Can I invite my friends to join my league?

Send this site to your mates and get them to sign up for early access. Once they’ve created an account you can search them in the app and create your own leagues.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

7. I signed up but haven’t heard back

We are slowly adding as many golf courses to the app as we can, but currently this is a manual process. It may take us some time to get to you, so please bear with us.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

8. How does the betting feature work, and is real money involved?

Honors allows you to bet on the real events that happen during your round (like who will beat who, or who’s getting trapped in the sand), but no real money is processed or transacted through the app itself. Settling up at the end is still up to you!
